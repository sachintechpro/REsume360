# REsume360 — Career & Market Value Demo

REsume360 is a resume-first, interactive career and salary assessment demonstration. It helps visitors understand their professional profile, explore potential roles and modeled salary ranges, review employer-direct career destinations, and create a shareable MyResume360 profile.

## How to use the demo

1. **Start with your resume.** Open the application and upload a PDF, DOCX or TXT resume, or paste resume text. Resume analysis runs in your browser. For PDF and DOCX, the browser loads document-reading libraries from a CDN.
2. **Review what REsume360 understood.** Check the extracted experience, capabilities, skills and role families. Correct or supplement the information before interpreting results.
3. **Add optional career context.** Enter relevant location, compensation range, preferences and other optional details when comfortable. You can explore the demo without supplying every optional field.
4. **Explore salary and role matches.** Review potential job titles, fit indications, estimated salary bands and hourly or consulting-rate scenarios. These are illustrative models, not verified offers or guaranteed market pay.
5. **Explore jobs directly with employers.** Open employer career pages and any listed official job links. Listings are examples or previously researched opportunities, not a real-time vacancy feed. Confirm each role is still open on the employer website.
6. **Generate MyResume360.** Create a standalone HTML professional profile that you can save and optionally host or share yourself. Generating a file does not publish it automatically.
7. **Explore financial education.** Review short introductions to retirement, investing, housing, insurance and related planning topics, with links to educational resources. This section is informational, not personalized financial advice.
8. **Export your results.** Use the available report or print options to keep a copy of the assessment for your own reference.

## What to expect

- **Privacy-first:** No account, server-side resume database or persistent browser storage is intentionally used by the demo. Resume and assessment information is processed locally in the active browser session.
- **Session behavior:** Refreshing or closing the page may clear your entered information; save any exported files you want to retain.
- **Third-party services:** Document-parsing libraries may be loaded from external CDNs, which can see normal network metadata. The demo does not intentionally upload resume contents to those CDNs.
- **Salary methodology:** Occupation benchmarks reference U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics data. Fit scoring, modeled salary ranges and contractor-rate multipliers are illustrative assumptions, not official BLS forecasts for a particular individual.
- **Job links:** Only official employer destinations are presented; availability is not continuously verified.
- **Sharing:** A MyResume360 HTML file remains on your device until you choose to distribute or publish it.

## Suggested demo walkthrough

Use a sample or non-sensitive resume. Upload it, inspect and correct the profile, compare two role options, open an employer's official career site, then export MyResume360. This illustrates the complete resume-to-career exploration journey.

## Important note

REsume360 is an educational and portfolio demonstration. It does not provide employment guarantees, professional tax advice, financial advice, or investment recommendations. Always verify job availability and consult qualified professionals for decisions that require personalized guidance.

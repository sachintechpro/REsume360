# REsume360 — Career & Market Value Demo

REsume360 is a resume-first, interactive career and salary assessment demonstration. It helps visitors understand their professional profile, explore potential roles and modeled salary ranges, review employer-direct career destinations, and create a shareable MyResume360 profile.

## How to use the demo

1. **Start with your resume.** Upload a PDF, DOCX or TXT file, or paste resume text. File parsing runs in your browser; PDF and DOCX parsing libraries load from a CDN.
2. **Review the profile.** Check the extracted summary, terms and role families. Correct them before using the estimates.
3. **Set optional preferences.** Add target titles, location and work preferences if they help you narrow the search.
4. **Explore role and salary signals.** Compare possible role families with illustrative salary ranges and employer-posted information. These are estimates, not a personal compensation offer.
5. **Use the Career toolkit.** Paste a job description to compare its terms with your resume, draft accomplishment bullets and STAR interview stories from your own examples, and create a downloadable 30-day plan.
6. **Check employer openings.** Open the official career pages, confirm the listing is current and review the requirements. REsume360 does not submit applications.
7. **Follow the page-end prompts.** Each section guides you back to the next useful step: resume review, role tools or employer listings.
8. **Review an opportunity as a whole.** Consider compensation, benefits, work expectations and your priorities before deciding whether to apply.
9. **Create MyResume360.** Draft a portable profile and download the HTML file to your device. It is not published automatically.
10. **Keep a copy if useful.** Use the available print or report options to save assessment details for your own reference.

## Feedback and account connections

After 60 seconds of visible use, an optional feedback and newsletter prompt appears once in the session. Visitors can also open it from the footer. The demo shows a confirmation, but does not send or save feedback or subscription choices. ChatGPT, Google and Apple sign-in are future options; this preview does not connect to those accounts.

## What to expect

- **Privacy-first:** No account, server-side resume database or persistent browser storage is intentionally used by the demo. Resume and assessment information is processed locally in the active browser session.
- **Session behavior:** Refreshing or closing the page may clear your entered information; save any exported files you want to retain.
- **Third-party services:** Document-parsing libraries may be loaded from external CDNs, which can see normal network metadata. The demo does not intentionally upload resume contents to those CDNs.
- **Salary methodology:** Occupation benchmarks reference U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics data. Fit scoring, modeled salary ranges and contractor-rate multipliers are illustrative assumptions, not official BLS forecasts for a particular individual.
- **Job links:** Only official employer destinations are presented; availability is not continuously verified.
- **Sharing:** A MyResume360 HTML file remains on your device until you choose to distribute or publish it.

## Suggested demo walkthrough

Use a sample or non-sensitive resume. Upload it, inspect and correct the profile, compare two role options, open an employer's official career site, then export MyResume360. This illustrates the complete resume-to-career exploration journey.

## Important note

REsume360 is an educational and portfolio demonstration. It does not provide employment guarantees, professional tax advice, financial advice, or investment recommendations. Always verify job availability and consult qualified professionals for decisions that require personalized guidance.
